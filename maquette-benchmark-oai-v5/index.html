BENCH 0.0 · Decision lab

The right model, in the right place.

One request. Evidence first. Decision only when justified.

OrchestrAI evaluates available evidence, constraints and execution context before recommending a model or infrastructure — and can refuse to decide when the evidence is insufficient.

Task-specific Evidence-based Cloud / Local / On-premise Recommend-only

FIG 0.1

Evidence layers

Every recommendation is grounded in explicit observations and constraints. Missing evidence remains missing.

  • Observed quality / fidelity
  • Latency
  • Cost knowledge
  • Privacy / sovereignty
  • Capability fit

FIG 0.2

Swappable intelligence

Models and execution targets remain interchangeable. OrchestrAI qualifies them per task instead of naming a permanent winner.

FIG 0.3

Decision gate

Options are progressively filtered. If none is sufficiently qualified, OrchestrAI stops instead of fabricating confidence.

  • Request
  • Constraints
  • Evidence
  • Eligible options
  • Decision

BENCH 0.2 · Why

Why this decision

No scores. No invented causality. Only what the engine knows, what blocks a recommendation, and what evidence comes next.

0.2.1

What OrchestrAI knows

  • Real observations exist
  • Some legal / documentary tests passed
  • Some tests failed
  • No global model ranking

0.2.2

What blocks the decision

  • Cost not measured
  • Task transfer not validated
  • Evidence remains synthetic / limited
  • One required criterion is not measured

0.2.3

Next evidence

  • Define measurable documentary-fidelity criterion
  • Measure cost
  • Validate transfer between tasks
  • Define evidence sufficiency rule

BENCH 0.3 · Method

How OrchestrAI decides

0.3.1

Traceable

Every outcome points back to explicit observations and constraints. What was examined stays visible; what was missing stays missing.

0.3.2

Task-specific

Qualification is per request and context. There is no permanent global winner across models or infrastructures.

0.3.3

Conservative

When evidence is insufficient, OrchestrAI refuses to recommend rather than fabricating confidence.

0.3.4

Human-governed

Recommend-only: the system proposes or withholds; a human remains responsible for the operational choice.
